How we list operators

Our editors open an account with each operator, test deposits and withdrawals, and check that the site's terms and account tools are easy to find. We add an operator to the directory only when its games, payments and account settings work as described, and we recheck every entry twice a year. Operators cannot pay for a place in the directory or for a higher position in the list, and entries appear in the order we reviewed them.
