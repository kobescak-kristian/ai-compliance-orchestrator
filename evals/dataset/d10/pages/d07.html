Preview: Ashcombe Rangers v Felsted Harriers

By Jonas Wehrle, Matchday Notes, Saturday 7 November 2026

Ashcombe Rangers host Felsted Harriers at Millbrook Park on Saturday afternoon, with both sides level on points near the top of the Western League table.

Rangers have won four of their last five home games, built on a settled back line and the pace of winger Tomas Reyes. Their manager, Elin Hartley, has named the same starting eleven for three matches in a row and is unlikely to change it now.

Harriers arrive after a long week of travel but with their best away record in years. Striker Callum Ostrowski has scored in each of his last three games, and the fans who make the trip north will expect him to test the Rangers defence early. A draw would keep both clubs in touch with the leaders.

Team news
  • Ashcombe Rangers: midfielder Ruben Tasker returns from a hamstring injury.
  • Felsted Harriers: goalkeeper Ivo Brandt is suspended after a red card last weekend.
  • Both clubs report no new injuries from training on Thursday.
